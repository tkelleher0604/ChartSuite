# QA: a paralegal's week

A pass through Rboreal as a paralegal on the fund legal team, in an ordinary week, on `production` at 34eb7c5 (October 25, 2026). Each day's work was done through the UI in headless Chrome, with state carried from page to page in one browser profile, and its effects checked on the other pages. No page threw a JavaScript error at any point.

Screenshots and the scripts that drove each step were in the QA session's scratch folder, `paralegal-qa\` (temporary, and not in the repository), with the screenshots in its `shots` folder. The harness there, `probe.sh`, copies a page beside itself with a script injected, screenshots it and deletes the copy. In the table, "Shot" names a file in that `shots` folder.

## Notes on the week

- **Monday.** Adding the SPV by keyboard in Records works well, apart from finding 1. Its Delaware Annual Tax Due (June 1, 2027) and Amount ($400) appeared at once, in the record, the Calendar (Later) and its Dates tab. Home showed it under Recent changes, Recently viewed and Coming up (its formation date).
- **Tuesday.** Entering the manager's Form ADV Last Filed as October 27, 2026 leaves Form ADV Due at March 31, 2027. That's right: FY2026's annual amendment is still due, and the Calendar's Why says how it's worked out. Entering an annual filing, March 15, 2027, moved it to March 30, 2028, leap year included. Both showed in the Calendar.
- **Wednesday.** A position can only be deleted, not ended, so the resignation leaves no "until" on the record (sample.md's known gap 2). Deleting asks first, because the position's Title and Start Date go with it. Undo, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z all worked on the same page. History and the record's History tab recorded each step, with Undo and Redo badges.

## What worked well

- **Pasting from Excel.** Five rows with a heading row were matched by heading. Yes and No became attested and ruled-out tags. It was one undoable step, and the toast said what it did ("Pasted 15 values and added 5 individuals").
- **Editing like a spreadsheet.** Typing over a cell fixed a typo, and Enter moved down. The filter chip reads as a sentence ("U.S. Person is Attested"), and the count updates ("8 of 12 individuals"). Sorting works. Export CSV has a byte-order mark and a sensible file name.
- **The record page.** The header shows kind, role and type. Computed values are in italics with fx. The Documents tab lists the new certificate, and Formation Document on File flipped to attested as soon as it was linked. Details become a tab at 1024 px.
- **Linking a document.** The dialog is clear. A pasted network path is taken as it is, and the name and Concerns are guessed from the file name.
- **Deadlines.** They're right and explained: Annual Tax Due and Amount for the new SPV, Form ADV moving on, and the Why panel showing the formula and what it read.
- **History.** Each change is told in a sentence with links. Like changes fold into a run. The record filter is kept in the address. "Restore this" works as a way back when Undo can't.
- **Navigation.** G then D works. Initials find people ("JSF"). Commands are in the palette (Export entities as CSV, New entity, Theme: Dark, Undo).
- **Dark mode and narrow windows.** All nine pages are clean in dark mode, with nothing illegible. No page scrolls sideways at 1024×768.
- **No JavaScript errors** on any page, all week.
