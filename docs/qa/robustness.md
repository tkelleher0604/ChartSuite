# Robustness QA

What a curious colleague might try that the happy path doesn't: what breaks, loses data, misleads or stalls. Tested on `production` at 34eb7c5 ("Don't count an investor group as a single owner for tax classification"), on 2026-10-25.

**How.** Headless Chrome (`--headless=new`, fresh profile per run, `--allow-file-access-from-files`), driving the real pages from a harness page in `app/` that loads them in iframes. Each test script clicks, types (setting values and dispatching input, change and key events), pastes (a `ClipboardEvent` with a `DataTransfer`), and reads the page and the model back. Page errors come from Chrome's console log. Two tabs are two iframes on one workbook, so the real `storage` events fire between them. Timings were taken in real time, without virtual time. For those, each page was copied with a timing script first in `<head>` and the edits run at the end of `<body>`.

The scripts and screenshots were in the QA session's scratch folder, `robust\` (temporary, and not in the repository). The test scripts are under `tests\`, the runner is `qa.sh` and the timing runner is `perf.sh`. Screenshots below are named by their file in that folder.

Severity, most severe first: **data loss**, **blocker for a demo**, **bad**, **annoying**, **polish**.

## What held up well

- **Escaping.** A name made of `<script>alert('x')</script><img src=x onerror="alert('img')"> "Fund" 'Q' &`, with accented letters, CJK, an emoji and a right-to-left override, is drawn as plain text on every page: Home, Chart, Record, Calendar, History, Documents, Schema and the page title. No alert fired and no element was injected. CSV export quotes commas, quotes and line breaks correctly.
- **A blank chart.** Every page's empty state is clear and offers one action. Building from nothing through the UI worked without an error:
  - the first kind and box, by double-clicking the sheet;
  - the first relation, by dragging a handle and creating General Partner/Fund in the Relate view;
  - the first entry, with Add › Field, whose value box said "Reads as a date" as March 4, 2019 was typed;
  - the first document, from a pasted EDGAR link on Documents.

  Switching to the sample and back kept everything. A deleted chart can be restored from the toast or the chart menu.
- **Deleting and restoring.** Deleting the main fund (118 dependent records), then Restore, gave back exactly the same state: 808 entered records, 1,312 in all, 504 computed. Renaming Formation Date at every location renamed it in all 3 formulas.
- **Undo and redo within a page.** After 40 edits, 45 undos landed exactly on the start and 45 redos exactly on the end, with "Nothing to undo" and "Nothing to redo" at the limits. Undo stays separate per chart when you switch charts.
- **Import rejects bad files** that aren't JSON, are empty, are the wrong shape or have no charts. It says why inline and leaves the stored workbook alone.
- **Most garbage addresses fail calmly**: "No record here", "… was deleted · Restore", "Definition not found", and the Calendar ignoring parameters it can't read.
- **Formula errors are visible where they land.** Home lists them, with their code and the records they hit. Record pages show `#CIRCULAR!` or `#DIV/0!` where the value would be. The editor underlines syntax errors as you type and refuses to save them ("Fix the formula first: Expected ')'").
- **Numbers and dates.** Ownership sums allow for floating-point error: 10% + 20% + 70% counts as adding up. Impossible dates (2024-02-30, Feb 29 2023, 31/12/2024) are refused with an example rather than misread. Money and percent parsing refuses `$1.5M`, `(1,000)`, `€500` and `12,5%` rather than guessing.
- **Paste on a small chart.** 200 rows went in as one change with one Undo, and the one value that didn't parse was named in the toast. (This was the sample alone; the 5-second slowdown with 200 more entities is #4.)
- **Keyboard.** Escape closes the palette, the menus, the dialogs and the shortcut sheet on every page. Focus rings show. The Records grid, the Calendar and Documents lists, and G-then-letter navigation all work from the keyboard.
- **Dark mode.** No text under 3:1 contrast on any page; the chart's sheet stays paper-white on purpose.
- **1024×768** has no sideways page scroll on any page.
- **Present mode** enters, steps through the views and exits on Esc, or when the browser leaves full screen.
- **Speed on the sample.** Each page's first load (parsing the scripts and the first render) takes 0.36–0.68 s. A typical edit takes 60–80 ms of model work (saving included), plus 5–30 ms to draw the page again. History is the slowest to redraw, at about 70 ms. An undo takes 60–130 ms.
