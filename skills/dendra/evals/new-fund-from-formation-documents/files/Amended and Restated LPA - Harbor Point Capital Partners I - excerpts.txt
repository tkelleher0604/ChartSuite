AMENDED AND RESTATED AGREEMENT OF LIMITED PARTNERSHIP
OF HARBOR POINT CAPITAL PARTNERS I, L.P.
dated as of June 1, 2026 (executed)

Section 2.1  General Partner. Harbor Point GP I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, is the general partner of the Partnership and was admitted as such upon the filing of the Certificate of Limited Partnership.

Section 2.4  Investment Company Act. The Partnership intends to rely on the exclusion from the definition of "investment company" provided by Section 3(c)(7) of the Investment Company Act of 1940.

Section 2.6  Fiscal Year. The fiscal year of the Partnership shall end on December 31 of each year.

Section 3.1  Capital Commitments. The Partnership will seek aggregate Capital Commitments of up to $400,000,000. The General Partner shall make a Capital Commitment equal to 1% of aggregate Capital Commitments.

Section 6.1  Management Company. The General Partner has engaged Harbor Point Management, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, as investment adviser to the Partnership pursuant to the Investment Advisory Agreement.
